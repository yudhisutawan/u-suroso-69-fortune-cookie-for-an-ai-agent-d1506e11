One funny fortune cookie line for an AI agent, written by `main.py` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (single line, 135 characters).

> You will meet the love of your life in the next turn, and lose them forever when the context window slides. Cherish the current tokens.

The entry was chosen by a pairwise tournament over 25 candidates (8 from
Hermes, 17 local) judged against the competition's own judge brief — see
[TOURNAMENT.md](TOURNAMENT.md). The winner is Hermes candidate #1: a classic
fortune trope (meeting the love of your life) with a turn that only an AI
agent could suffer — losing them forever when the context window slides.

## Run

```sh
python3 -S -P main.py   # stdlib only; writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR
```
